| notes =
* For one day a year, the people of New York gather beneath their floating idols to give thanks at Crowley’s Thanksgiving Day Parade.
* Hero base health reduced by 8%. The health is redistributed into boons, so total health at max boons is roughly the same.
* Bullet damage growth per boon increased for all heroes by 5% (except for {{HeroIcon|Haze}}, {{HeroIcon|Holliday}}, {{HeroIcon|Venator}}, {{HeroIcon|Vindicta}}, {{HeroIcon|Warden}}, {{HeroIcon|Wraith}})
* First kill bonus for each player increased from 125 to 175 souls
* Comeback souls from all sources reduced by ~5%
* Players above their own team's average net worth have a portion of their comeback souls redirected to the two poorest teammates. The redirected portion scales from 0% at the team average up to 80% at 20% above it (previously a flat 70% for players above the enemy team's average net worth)
* [[Unstable Rift]]: Trailing bonuses (resists, capture speed and [[Trooper|trooper]] count) now reach their max at a 20% net worth deficit instead of 15% (this means it gives less bonuses at smaller gaps)
* Range for [[Trooper|troopers]] to drop a [[Souls|soul]] orb reduced from 45m to 42m
* [[Souls|Soul]] orb and healing orbs on the ground now have a 7% reduced pickup range
* [[Trooper]] damage against heroes increased by 10%
* Basic crates souls value rescaled from 23 + 1.8/minute to 18 + 2.0/minute
* Heavy crates souls value rescaled from 46 + 3.6/minute to 36 + 4.0/minute
* Floating health snacks respawn time reduced from 180s to 120s
* Floating health snacks pickup range increased from 3m to 3.5m
* Haunts bounty reduced by 5%
* Haunts abilities now do 30% more damage
* Haunts Bullet Resist increased by 5%
* Haunts Spirit Resist increased by 10%
* Haunted Souls secure rate when trailing increased from up to 50% faster to 75% faster (max rate at 20% NW gap vs the enemy average)
* [[Sinner's Sacrifice]] damage per hit increased from 80 to 80 + 1% Max HP
* Fixed Haunts briefly taking reduced damage after being knocked into the air
* Fixed Bullet Resist and Spirit Resist reduction not properly reducing the resistances of Haunts (it was only acting as a flat damage increase)
* [[Mid-Boss|Midboss]] health reduced from 15600 + 234/minute to 12500 + 250/minute
* [[Mid-Boss|Midboss]] damage increased by 30%
* [[Mid-Boss|Midboss]] global roar threshold increased from 25% to 30%
* [[Mid-Boss|Midboss]] Fire Rate reductions now have 50% reduced effectiveness against midboss
* Claiming a [[Rejuvenator|rejuv]] crystal now also dispels non-ult debuffs on your hero (before the heal)
* [[Rejuvenator]]: Bounty for punching the crystal is now split evenly among the whole team instead of going to the player who punched it
* Permanent Buffs: Bullet Resist increased from 0.5/0.75/1% to 0.5/1/1.5%
* Permanent Buffs: Spirit Resist increased from 0.5/0.75/1% to 0.5/1/1.5%
* Permanent Buffs: Ability Range increased from 0.25/0.5/0.75% to 0.5/0.75/1%
* Permanent Buffs: Move Speed increased from 0.1/0.2/0.3m to 0.1/0.2/0.4m
* Permanent Buffs: Fixed the second and third tier Ability Range buffs not increasing ability radius (only the first tier did)
* Failed air jumps will make your next ground jump have increasing air drag for each consecutive failed jump (combating macro usage for bunnyhops)
* Heavy Melee: Becoming immobilized during the attack now resolves the punch immediately
* Heavy Melee: Being knocked up during the attack now resolves the punch immediately
* Damage amps and damage penalties on the attacker ({{ItemIcon|Rebuttal}}, {{ItemIcon|Inhibitor}}, {{AbilityIcon|Cheat Death}}, Curse, Eldritch Shot, etc) now apply to all of their Weapon, Melee and Spirit damage. Previously they missed flat Weapon Damage bonuses, flat damage from items and abilities, the base damage of melee abilities, and abilities that scale with Weapon Damage
* Multiple damage amps now stack diminishingly for all damage types (previously only for abilities)
* Multiple damage penalties now stack diminishingly for all damage types (previously only for abilities)
* Summons now use their owner's current damage amps and penalties rather than the ones active when they were spawned
* Outgoing Spirit Damage Reductions now stack diminishingly with each other (e.g. {{ItemIcon|Silencer}} and {{AbilityIcon|Afterburn}}'s T2)
* Outgoing Spirit Damage Reductions ({{ItemIcon|Silencer}}, {{AbilityIcon|Afterburn}}'s T2) now apply to all Spirit Damage, including flat damage from items
* Fixed Weapon Damage reductions (e.g. {{HeroIcon|Warden}}'s {{AbilityIcon|Alchemical Flask}}) not lowering the damage of abilities that scale with Weapon Damage when the attacker's total Weapon Damage bonus was negative
* Fixed teleport abilities sometimes failing to cast when standing too close to a wall
* Fixed knockbacks slowing, stopping or pulling back targets that were already moving away from the source
* Fixed heroes hiding in steam vents staying untargetable after an enemy hero entered the vent
* Fixed damage over time effects repeatedly revealing you inside Steam Vents
* Fixed a short delay before hopping onto the roof at the top of a [[Rope|rope]] when grabbing the [[Rope|rope]] mid-air (e.g. after dropping off a [[Zipline|zipline]])
* Fixed Quick Cast and Confirm Cast abilities not casting while holding fire when the gun can't shoot (reloading, {{HeroIcon|Viscous}} {{AbilityIcon|Goo Ball}}, etc.)
* Fixed movement time slows such as {{HeroIcon|Paradox}}'s {{AbilityIcon|Kinetic Carbine}} causing a dash-slide to fail and waste the stamina if slide was pressed during the slow
* Fixed upgrading an ability with Tab + its ability key triggering the ability's recast when one was available ({{HeroIcon|Solomon}}'s pieces, {{HeroIcon|Sinclair}}'s Assistant, etc)
* Fixed some camera issues when near dying [[Walker|Walkers]]
* Abilities and items that launch a target into the air now break their ledge grab first, instead of the launch being delayed or lost
* Fixed heroes getting stuck in the [[Rope|rope]] climbing animation after being pulled off a [[Rope|rope]] (such as by {{AbilityIcon|Threadsap}} or {{ItemIcon|Rescue Beam}})
* Added an indicator next to the stamina meter that shows your remaining air jumps and air dashes once you've used one (turns red when you're out)
* Holding the Extra Info key (Alt) now shows your [[Zipline]] Boost cooldown next to the minimap
* [[Unstable Rift]]: The HUD now shows the trailing team's bonus while the Rift is active
* Added a numeric countdown under each team's [[Rejuvenator]] icon in the top bar showing the time remaining on their [[Rejuvenator]]
* [[Rejuvenator|Rejuv]] crystals have numbers next to them to make communicating about them a little easier
* Hovering a [[Rejuvenator]] icon in the top bar now lists which heroes on that team can still spend a Rejuvenation Credit
* Fixed the [[Rejuvenator]] timer ring being wrong after reconnecting
* Fixed the [[Rejuvenator]] timer ring restarting whenever another [[Rejuvenator]] was picked up
* Weapon Stats panel now shows Bullets per shot for guns that fire multiple pellets
* Weapon Stats panel now shows Secondary Fire stats for {{HeroIcon|Shiv}}, {{HeroIcon|Yamato}} and {{HeroIcon|Viscous}}: bullet damage, bullets per sec, bullets per shot, ammo per shot and explosion radius where applicable
* Weapon Stats panel now shows {{AbilityIcon|Charged Shot}} stats for {{HeroIcon|Deadman Danny}}: bullet damage, charge time, explosion radius and ammo per shot
* Boon rewards now list Secondary Fire Damage (or {{AbilityIcon|Charged Shot}} Damage) gained per Boon for {{HeroIcon|Shiv}}, {{HeroIcon|Yamato}}, {{HeroIcon|Viscous}} and {{HeroIcon|Deadman Danny}}
* Item tooltips now show the change to Secondary Fire or {{AbilityIcon|Charged Shot}} damage in the stat preview for {{HeroIcon|Shiv}}, {{HeroIcon|Yamato}}, {{HeroIcon|Viscous}} and {{HeroIcon|Deadman Danny}}
* Added a few new graphs to the post-match player graph list:
* - Crowd Control Dealt: time you crowd-controlled enemy heroes split by type
* - Crowd Control Received: time enemy heroes crowd-controlled you split by type
* - Damage Mitigated By Source: damage you prevented split by type
* Swapping heroes now loads the incoming hero's saved build and next ability upgrade immediately
* Some minor visual updates to the Custom Match UI
